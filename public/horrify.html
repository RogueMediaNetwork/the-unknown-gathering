// Competition Tracks
CHOOSE YOUR PATH
Track One
Horrify a Classic
Take an existing movie and transform it into a horror film. The film must clearly reference or parody the original source while presenting a substantially new horror interpretation.
  • Titanic → zombie survival story
  • Caddyshack → slasher film
  • The Wizard of Oz → psychological horror
Track Two
Horrify an Urban Legend
Create a completely original urban legend centered around a real Texas city, town, landmark, or location. Must be presented as found footage, recovered footage, or shockumentary style.
  • The Platypus of Plata
  • A haunted Texas water tower
  • Big Tex as a supernatural entity
  • A forgotten roadside attraction

// Competition Rules
THE LAW
⏱
10-Minute Limit
Films must be no longer than 10 minutes including credits. No exceptions.
📅
Made During Competition
Films must be substantially created during the official twelve-day competition window beginning after the Kickoff Mixer.
🎬
Format: MP4 / H.264
MP4 (preferred), H.264 encoded, 1080p resolution recommended. Files that cannot be screened may be disqualified.
📄
Required Paperwork
Talent & crew release forms, location agreements, music releases, and any applicable stock footage licenses must be obtained by the filmmaker.
▾ Click to view & download forms
⚖️
Content Restrictions
No unlicensed copyrighted music/footage, unauthorized trademarks, pornographic content, real illegal activity, or material violating applicable law.
🏆
Judging Criteria
Creativity, storytelling, horror elements, originality, technical execution, and use of competition theme. All judging decisions are final.
📺
Screening Rights
By submitting, participants grant organizers permission to screen the film for competition-related events. Filmmakers retain full ownership of their work.
🔧
Your Own Resources
Filmmakers supply all equipment, cast, crew, locations, props, costumes, post-production resources, and release forms.
